# Contributing to CIVITAS Ω

Contributions focused on deterministic tests, documentation, policy verification, provenance integrity and bounded scientific evidence are welcome. Provide the exact modified paths, cited upstream documentation, commands actually executed and a typed test/evidence verdict.

Do not submit credentials, personal datasets, invasive surveillance code, rate-limit bypasses or unqualified claims of formal proof. Proposed new providers must be staged and independently reviewed for terms, quotas, attribution, data freshness and purpose.

A passing local test is not a production or scientific assertion. Changes must preserve negative evidence and open gates.
